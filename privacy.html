Privacy policy — Bridge: Contract Club

Effective 24 October 2026. Developer: Dmytro Husiev.

Offline play

Saved sessions, preferences and statistics remain on your device. The app has no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking, purchases or developer-operated account or game server. Standard iOS backups and platform diagnostics follow your Apple settings.

Online play and Apple Game Center

Online play is optional and uses Apple Game Center for authentication, matchmaking, invitations and game transport. Participants see your Game Center display name. The app uses game-scoped player IDs in memory to assign seats and validate commands. Game actions and player-private game snapshots are sent to the participants; the host device holds all hands, while other devices receive their own hand and exposed dummy. Online matches are not saved by the app after exit. The developer does not operate a service that stores those online identities or matches.

Apple independently processes Game Center information under Apple’s Game Center privacy policy. Game Center profile visibility is controlled in Apple settings. This policy does not claim that no data is transmitted during online play.

Support and removal

External support links contact GitHub under its own privacy policy. Issues you choose to post are public. Support information is used to investigate the issue. Do not post sensitive information. Delete the app and its data to remove local sessions and settings; Game Center data is managed through Apple. Contact the developer.
